Privacy policy

Family Health Planner (the app), last updated 8 November 2026.

The short version

Everything you put into the app stays on your phone. The app has no accounts, no server, no advertising and no analytics. We (the people who make the app) never receive your information.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps what you enter so it can make and show your plan:

  • the names of the people in your family and their About me answers, which can include age, height, weight, health conditions, medicines, allergies, injuries and other health details;
  • your plan, your ticks and meal ratings, your shopping list, and the weigh-ins you add;
  • your settings.

This is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it. It is removed when you delete the app, or when you tap Delete everything in Settings.

Your phone's own backup may include the app's data: iCloud or computer backups on iPhone, and Google's device backup on Android. Those backups are run by Apple or Google under their own privacy terms, and you can turn them off in your phone's settings.

Backup files

If you tap Save a backup, the app makes a file with all of the above and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or email). Anyone who has that file can read it, so keep it somewhere private.

The internet

The app doesn't need the internet and doesn't send your information anywhere. If you tap a store link on the shopping list (for example "Search Coles"), it opens that store's website in your browser, and the search words (the item's name) go to that website. That's all.

Children

Adults can add their children and answer About me questions for them. The information stays on the phone, as above. The app isn't meant to be used by children on their own.

Health information

The app is a planning tool, not a medical device, and doesn't give medical advice. Please check your meals and exercise with your GP, dietitian or physio.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/scoregie/family-health-planner-app/issues.
