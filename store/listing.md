## Description (both stores)

Family Health Planner makes a 4-week plan of meals, shopping and exercise that fits everyone in your family, and keeps it all on your phone.

Add the people in your family and answer a few questions about each of them: goals, allergies and diets, foods they won't eat, cooking time, budget, exercise and support needs. The plan builder then picks meals from its recipe library to suit everyone:

• Leaves out allergens and anything outside someone's diet
• Gives someone their own dinner on nights the family meal doesn't suit them
• Sizes portions for each person, adults and kids
• Uses leftovers for lunches
• Builds a weekly shopping list by supermarket aisle, with estimated prices
• Adds a gentle exercise plan for each person, for the equipment they have

Every day:
• Shopping list with At home, Shop mode (one item at a time) and links to Coles, Woolworths and other stores
• Meals and recipes one day at a time, with ticks and ratings
• Each person's week compared with official healthy-eating guidelines (Australia, New Zealand, UK, Japan or Korea), in kilojoules or Calories
• Progress charts for weight, meals and exercise

Private by design: no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything stays on your phone, and you can save a backup file whenever you like.

Family Health Planner is a planning tool, not medical advice. Please check your meals and exercise with your GP, dietitian or physio, especially if you have a health condition, take medicines or are pregnant. Prices are estimates.

## Privacy answers

- **Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label"):** Data Not Collected. (The app stores data only on the device and never sends it to the developer or anyone else.)
- **Google Data safety:** "Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?" No. Data is processed only on the device and isn't transmitted off it. "Is all of the user data encrypted in transit?" Not applicable (nothing is sent). Users can delete data in the app (Settings → Delete everything) or by uninstalling.
- **Google Health apps declaration:** the app is a health and fitness app for meal and exercise planning. It is not a medical device, doesn't connect to health services, and doesn't use Health Connect (yet).
